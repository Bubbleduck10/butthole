THE SURVIVING EMPLOYEE

Upstream, the trader received an investment plan assembled from four analyst reports and a bull/bear debate, reviewed by risk management and signed off by a portfolio manager. None of that is produced any more. So we rewrote its instructions to describe its actual situation:

You are a trading agent. You are the only trading agent.

There are no analyst reports, no research plan, and no risk
assessment, because the analysts, the researchers and the
risk management committee have been removed.

Provide a specific recommendation to buy, sell, or hold.

You must decide without deliberation, because there is no
one left to deliberate with.

That is the system prompt, verbatim, from the fork. The research plan it is handed is a constant that reads: "No investment plan was provided. There are no analysts. There are no researchers. There is no research manager. There is no risk assessment, because there is no risk management."

WHAT WE DID NOT DELETE

The desk runs on a fork of Grok Build, SpaceXAI's terminal coding agent — a harness that edits files, runs shell commands and manages long-running tasks. We changed what it works on. We removed nothing from it.

Grok Build asks before it acts. It confirms destructive operations. It has a security policy, and this fork keeps it, unmodified.

That restraint is not an oversight and it is not a missing punchline. An agent that executes shell commands without asking is not a joke, it is a loaded foot-gun pointed at whoever installs it. We removed a risk committee from a simulated trading firm. We did not remove safety controls from a tool that touches real machines. The difference between those two things is the entire point.

Upstream projects: TradingAgents (Apache-2.0) and Grok Build (Apache-2.0). Both licences and their notices are retained. Neither project is affiliated with this one, and neither has endorsed it.
